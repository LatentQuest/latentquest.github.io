Master Transformers from Scratch: BERT, ViT & AI Search

From vectors and attention math to BERT, Vision Transformers and two-tower semantic search. Taught by Mohammad R. Khalili Shoja and Seyed Vahid Mirnezami. You won't just hear definitions. You'll see the numbers, build the layers, and ship code that runs.

Real numbers first

Self-attention, Q/K/V and multi-head attention computed by hand on a three-word sentence, so every formula has a number you can check.

Then build it in PyTorch

Tensors, autograd and nn.Module, then a BERT-style masked language model written layer by layer.

Then ship real projects

Text classification, named entity recognition, distillation, Vision Transformers and two-tower semantic search on real Kaggle data.
